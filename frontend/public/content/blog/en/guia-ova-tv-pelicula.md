# OVA, TV, ONA and movie: what each format means

Format labels help explain how a production was released, but they do not determine its quality or whether it belongs to the main story.

## TV

A series produced for television broadcast or equivalent episodic distribution. It usually has multiple episodes.

## OVA

Original Video Animation. Historically released directly for home video and often used for side stories, independent adaptations or special episodes.

## ONA

Original Net Animation. First published online or through a digital platform.

## Movie

A feature-length production. It may be standalone, a continuation or a compilation.
