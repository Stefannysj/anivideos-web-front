# Guide to following seasonal anime releases

Anime releases are often organized around yearly seasons. To follow them clearly, separate three pieces of information: start date, airing status and format.

## What to check before starting

- Confirm whether the series is airing, finished or still upcoming.
- Distinguish TV, movie, OVA, ONA and special formats; not all of them follow a weekly schedule.
- Verify the official platform available in your region.
- Treat ratings as a reference, not as a guarantee of personal taste.
