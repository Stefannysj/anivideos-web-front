# Editorial ranking: legal sources to discover what to watch

This ranking does not rate services by catalog quality. It prioritizes usefulness for verifying information before watching a work.

## 1. Official production website

When available, it is the most direct source for announcements, dates and promotional material.

## 2. Official streaming platform

Crunchyroll, Netflix, Viki and other services publish availability by country. A title can vary by region and date.

## 3. AniList

Useful for structured anime metadata, formats, studios, episode counts and external links.

## 4. TMDB

Provides movie and television information, images, videos and availability metadata. TMDB watch-provider data is supplied by JustWatch.

## 5. Official YouTube channels

Trailers from distributors, studios and platforms provide promotional material without relying on unauthorized copies.
